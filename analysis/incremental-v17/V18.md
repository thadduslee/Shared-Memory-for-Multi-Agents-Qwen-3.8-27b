# V17 live failure and V18 correction

V17 did not meet the user's goal. Its local tests and host-only speedups did not
prove useful, fast live answers. The failed full run is preserved at
`runs/gatemem-medical-v17-openrouter-full-20261101T231136+0800`.
At its final export, all four utility questions were unanswered; five checkpoints
were scored in total. Mean source latency was 18.07 seconds. About 97.6% of source
wall time was spent in model/API requests. There were 22 quarantined events. The process was
interrupted during a subsequent source, so one job remains running in the ledger;
exit-status.json records the actual terminal process outcome.
